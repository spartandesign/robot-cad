Custom Printed Parts

Sometimes the kit doesn’t have the part you need: a sensor mount, a spacer, or a bracket at a strange angle. This module teaches you to design printed parts that bolt onto V5 metal and fit the first time.

🛠️ PLTW ProjectModule 2 · set by your coach

A PLTW Project asks you to use skills you already have to make something real, with clear requirements and limits.

🎯 Your goal

  • I can design a printed part with holes on the 0.5 in pattern.
  • I can choose a screw clearance hole by testing, not guessing.
  • I can use variables so one change updates the whole part.

🧰 You’ll need

  • Onshape and calipers
  • A #8-32 screw and a piece of V5 metal for test fitting
  • Your coach prints the parts
  • The bracket designer on this page

⏳ Time and limits

  • 2 team meetings, about 60 minutes each, plus print time between them.
  • Check the current game manual: custom parts have rules, and they change every season.
  • Holes on the 0.5 in pattern. At least two screws, so the part can’t spin.
  • Prints flat, with no supports.

Step-by-step plan

Follow the steps in order. The minutes are a guide. Check off each checkpoint before you move on.

Meeting 1 · about 60 min

  1. 10 minWatch the video.
  2. 15 minDesign a hole-test coupon: three holes at 0.170, 0.180, and 0.190 in.
  3. 20 minPlan your part on the bracket designer. Copy the hole centers.
  4. 15 minStart modeling with variables: #pitch = 0.5 in, #hole = your best guess.

✔ Checkpoint: Test coupon submitted for printing. Part plan done.

Meeting 2 · about 60 min

  1. 15 minTest the coupon with a real #8-32 screw. Pick the hole that fits.
  2. 5 minChange the #hole variable to the winner. Watch every hole update.
  3. 25 minFinish the part. Check it prints flat, with no supports.
  4. 15 minExport, submit, and take the self-check.

✔ Checkpoint: Your part uses the tested hole size and is ready to print.

Use the smallest one that fits without forcing. Put your tested hole size in a variable. Change that one number, and every hole in the part updates at once.

Print it, or use the kit?

Use kit metal when it does the job: it’s strong and it’s legal. Print a part when you need a shape the kit doesn’t have, like a sensor mount, a spacer, or a bracket at an odd angle. Before you design, check the current V5RC game manual. It says what custom parts are allowed, and the rules change every season.

Design on the pattern

  • Holes on the grid. Put every screw hole on the 0.5 in pattern, so it lines up with the metal.
  • One screw is a hinge. A part held by one screw can spin. Use at least two, and put a hole out of line if the part gets pushed sideways.
  • Screw size. V5 uses #8-32 screws. The thread is 0.164 in across, so the hole must be bigger. Printed holes come out a little small, so start around 0.180 in and test.
  • Strength. Print it flat so the layers run along the part, and keep walls thick enough to take a hit.

Test the hole before you trust it

Print a small coupon with three holes: 0.170, 0.180, and 0.190 in. Push a real screw through each one. Use the smallest hole the screw passes through without forcing. That’s your team’s number for this printer.

Try it: design a bracket

Tap the grid to place screw holes. The tool gives you the plate size and every hole center, ready to type into Onshape.

Build it in Onshape

Goal: one number controls every hole

Make variables

In a new Part Studio, add two variables: #pitch = 0.5 in and #hole = 0.180 in.

✅ Check it: both variables show at the top of the feature list.

Goal: holes exactly on the pattern

Sketch the plate and hole centers

Sketch the plate outline. Place hole centers using the numbers from the bracket designer, and dimension them with #pitch.

✅ Check it: every hole center is a multiple of #pitch from the corner.

Goal: the part

Extrude and cut the holes

Extrude the plate. Cut the holes using #hole for the diameter.

✅ Check it: change #hole to 0.190 in. Every hole grows at once. Change it back.

Goal: a part that prints

Check and export

Lay the biggest flat face on the plate. No overhangs past 45°. Export and submit.

✅ Check it: no red features, no supports needed.
