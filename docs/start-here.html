Getting Started

Start Here: How the Team Uses CAD

This book is the CAD track for the second semester of Competitive Robotics, Period 2 (Spartan Design Robotics, Team 2822), January 5 to June 4, 2027. Here’s the rhythm:

  • Homework: one or two Onshape CAD for Robotics Competitions lessons each week.
  • Class: build sessions. Apply the lesson to our V5 robots, using this book’s module for that week.
  • Team milestones: the CAD Skills Check, the WorldsBot kickoff and design review, and engineering-notebook checkpoints stay in Canvas. The semester calendar shows how they line up.

Before Module 1

  • Onshape basics. You should be able to sketch, dimension, extrude, and mate. If you took Model & Make, Chapters 4–9 cover it. If not, do Onshape’s free Intro to CAD Unit 1 and Unit 2 first.
  • Onshape’s robotics course. Your weekly homework comes from Onshape’s free CAD for Robotics Competitions path, starting with Intro to Onshape in Week 1. If you did a lot of CAD in the fall, the first lessons will go fast.
  • Calipers. You’ll check CAD against real parts. Review Model & Make Chapter 1 if you need to.

How the team works in Onshape

  • Name documents by team, squad, and part: Team2822X_Drivetrain.
  • Share every document with your squad and your coach.
  • Branch when you try something risky. Merge when it works.
  • Save named versions (v1, v2, …) at every milestone. Judges love seeing how a design changed.
  • Your coach runs the 3D printers and the lasers. You submit files.

Two CAD tools

  • Onshape is our CAD platform for the robot: every part, assembly, and drawing.
  • TinkerCAD is only for quick flat parts going to the laser (pit signs, jigs, spacers, mockups), because it exports SVG files that both of our lasers read. See Module 6.

Your main course: Onshape CAD for Robotics

Onshape’s CAD for Robotics Competitions path teaches the CAD skills. This book shows how to use them with VEX V5 parts, our printers and laser, and the engineering notebook. Each module lists its homework lessons, week by week. Sign in with your school Onshape account so your progress saves. The lessons cover FTC, FRC, and VEX. When a lesson has a VEX version of its Onshape document, use that one, and always use the V5 library for parts.

The engineering notebook

Every module ends with a 📓 For your engineering notebook box. Add those pages the same day, while you remember why you made each choice. CAD screenshots, drawings, test numbers, and the game-manual rules you checked are all strong notebook evidence.
