Onshape Glossary

Key terms from Onshape’s CAD for Robotics Competitions units. Linked terms open Onshape Help.

TermMeaningUnit
CADComputer-aided design.Intro to Onshape
View cubeThe cube in the top right of the graphics area. Use it to change the view.Intro to Onshape
Onshape documentA collection of design data, organized into tabs.Intro to Onshape
Part StudioA tab where you build one or more parts.Intro to Onshape
AssemblyA tab that positions parts and defines how they move.Intro to Onshape
NormalPerpendicular to something.Intro to Onshape
PlaneA flat 2D surface that goes on forever.Intro to Onshape
MatePositions parts in an assembly and sets how they move.Assemble a Drivetrain
FixLocks a part in place so it can’t move.Assemble a Drivetrain
Mate connectorA small coordinate system that mates use to line parts up.Assemble a Drivetrain
Fastened mateJoins two parts with no movement at all.Assemble a Drivetrain
Revolute mateLets a part spin around one axis, and nothing else.Assemble a Drivetrain
Cylindrical mateLets a part spin and slide along one axis.Assemble a Drivetrain
Parts libraryA collection of standard parts for one robot platform, like VEX V5.Assemble a Drivetrain
SketchLines, curves, and points on a plane, with dimensions and constraints.Creating Custom Components
ConstrainedFully controlled: it can’t change unless you change a number.Creating Custom Components
ConstructionSketch lines that guide other geometry but don’t make solids.Creating Custom Components
DimensionA set length or angle in a sketch.Creating Custom Components
ExtrudeAdds depth to a sketch region.Creating Custom Components
FilletRounds a sharp edge.Creating Custom Components
HoleMakes standard holes at sketch points or mate connectors.Creating Custom Components
In contextEditing a part while seeing the assembly around it.Creating Custom Components
Use (project)Copies an existing edge onto your sketch plane.Creating Custom Components
MirrorReflects geometry across a line or plane.Creating Custom Components
ShareLets others view, edit, or copy a document.Collaboration
VersionA snapshot or save point you create.Collaboration
BranchA separate workspace for trying a different design.Collaboration
WorkspaceAn active, editable modeling space in a document.Collaboration
CompareShows the differences between versions or workspaces.Collaboration
MergeBrings changes from a branch or version into your workspace.Collaboration
CommentA message inside a document, with tags and mentions.Collaboration
DerivedBrings parts or sketches from one Part Studio into another.Collaboration
SlicingTurning a 3D model into printer instructions.Manufacturing
STLA common 3D file of a part’s shape.Manufacturing
KerfThe width of material a laser burns away.Manufacturing
DXFA common 2D vector file for laser and CNC cutting.Manufacturing
STEPA 3D file format many CAM programs read.Manufacturing
Additive manufacturingBuilding a part layer by layer, like a 3D printer.Manufacturing
Subtractive manufacturingCutting away material from a bigger piece.Manufacturing
ToleranceHow much a size is allowed to vary and still work.Manufacturing
PrototypingMaking test samples to try an idea.Manufacturing
DrawingA technical drawing with the sizes needed to make a part.Communicating Your Design
Drawing templateA preset drawing layout, for example for laser cutting.Communicating Your Design
Bill of materialsA table listing every part needed to build an assembly.Communicating Your Design
Named viewA saved camera angle and zoom.Communicating Your Design
ImportBringing other files into Onshape.Communicating Your Design
Link sharingA view-only link that works without an Onshape account.Communicating Your Design
