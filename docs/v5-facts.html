V5 Quick Facts

The numbers this book uses, with where they come from. When in doubt, measure the real part with calipers.

ItemValueNote
Hole spacing (pitch)0.5 inVEX counts sizes in pitch; 16 pitch = 8 in.
Screws#8-32, 0.164 in threadPrinted clearance holes: start near 0.180 in and test.
Standard shaft⅛ in squareWorks with standard motion parts.
High-strength shaft¼ in squareWon’t pass through the square holes in metal; needs HS parts.
Gears12, 36, 60, 84 teethStandard and high-strength gears mesh with each other.
Motor cartridgesRed 100 · Green 200 · Blue 600 RPMRed for torque, green for drivetrains, blue for speed.

Formulas

  • Gear ratio = driven teeth : driving teeth (VEX’s convention)
  • Output RPM = motor RPM ÷ ratio · Output torque = motor torque × ratio
  • Compound ratio = stage 1 ratio × stage 2 ratio
  • Wheel speed (in/s) = RPM × π × wheel diameter ÷ 60
  • Laser hole: draw it at (hole you need − kerf)
