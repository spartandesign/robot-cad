Why a robot team uses CAD

Building straight from your head works until two parts want the same space. CAD lets you find that problem on a screen, where fixing it costs nothing. It also gives your engineering notebook clear pictures of your design, which judges look for.

Get the official V5 parts

Onshape publishes an official VEX V5 Parts Library. There are two ways to use it:

  • Label the documents. Find the public V5 documents owned by Onshape and add them to a label named VEX V5 Parts. In an Assembly, choose Insert → Other documents to use them.
  • Use the V5 Library App. It appears on the right side of the screen and lets you search, configure, and insert parts.

Many parts are configurable. A C-channel, for example, lets you pick its length before you insert it. Right-click a placed part to change its configuration later.

The 0.5 inch rule

V5 metal has square holes every 0.5 in. VEX counts sizes in “pitch,” one pitch per hole. So a frame 16 holes wide is 16 × 0.5 = 8 in. Count holes, then multiply by 0.5. Always check a real part with calipers too.

Build a drivetrain frame

Goal: a plan before the parts

Sketch it first

On paper, draw two side rails and two cross members. Write the width and length in holes, then in inches.

✅ Check it: your sketch has both numbers, and they follow the 0.5 in rule.

Goal: real parts on screen

Insert the side rails

In a new Assembly, insert two C-channels from the V5 library. Set their length in holes to match your sketch.

✅ Check it: each channel shows the hole count you planned.

Goal: parts locked together

Fasten on hole centers

Fasten each cross member with a mate connector on a hole center. Fix the first rail so the frame has something to hold on to.

✅ Check it: drag a part. Nothing moves, because everything is fastened.

Goal: proof it’s square

Measure it

Use the Measure tool to check width and length, and both diagonals.

✅ Check it: the two diagonals match. If they don’t, a part is one hole off.

📓 For your engineering notebook

  • A screenshot of the frame, with width and length in holes and in inches.
  • Your real C-channel measurement next to the CAD value.
  • One sentence: what CAD caught that you would have missed.
