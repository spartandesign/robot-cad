Build your robot on the screen before you build it in metal. The second-semester CAD track for Period 2 Competitive Robotics: weekly Onshape lessons at home, and seven modules in class that take you from the V5 parts library to gear trains, custom printed and laser-cut parts, moving mechanisms, TinkerCAD laser files, and a subsystem your squad will actually use.

How to use this book

Every module gives you a mission card with your goal and limits, its Onshape homework, a session-by-session plan, a narrated video, a read-aloud, a practice tool, step-by-step tutorials, an engineering-notebook checklist, and a self-check.

Main course: Onshape’s free CAD for Robotics Competitions learning path. Each module lists its lessons, and the semester calendar shows every week.
